third reviewer next to the two judge models, not a substitute for the independent human check:
the same assistant that built the harness wrote this, it may share the stylistic preferences of
the models it is ranking, and it read each piece once. Labels are the packet's per-prompt
shuffled letters; the reasons are what I wrote down while reading.
